Drop the Needle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

The short version

Drop the Needle is a standalone music trivia game. The iOS app does not require an account and does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data through the app.

Data collection

The Drop the Needle iOS app does not collect personal information. It does not include advertising, third-party analytics, tracking technologies, or location services.

Game activity

Answers, scores, and game activity are used only to run the game. They are not transmitted to the developer.

Third-party services

The iOS app does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect information about you.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in a future version, this privacy policy will be updated to describe those changes.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to maria@mariaworld.com.
